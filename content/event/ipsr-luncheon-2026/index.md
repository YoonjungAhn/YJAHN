abstract: 'Real estate data can reveal far more than property values. In this talk, I will highlight a series of studies that use property records to uncover patterns of climate adaptation and long-term changes in the built environment. I will discuss the development of U.S. air-conditioning prevalence datasets to understand heat vulnerability, as well as historical settlement datasets reconstructed from real estate records. Together, these projects demonstrate how unconventional data sources can open new avenues for studying climate, the environment, and society.'
