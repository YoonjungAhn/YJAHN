abstract: A retrospective cohort analysis of Florida birth certificates examining associations
  of trimester-specific heatwave exposure (under several heatwave definitions) and PM2.5
  with preterm birth and term low birthweight, and whether air conditioning modifies these
  associations. Higher first-trimester PM2.5 was associated with greater preterm birth risk
  (aRR 1.010, 95% CI 1.008–1.012), and more heatwave days in the first trimester were associated
  with a slightly higher preterm birth risk.
