abstract: This study quantifies spatial patterns of power outage exposure among older
  adults across the United States from 2014 to 2021 using county-level outage data
  covering 3,108 counties. We measure outage duration, frequency, and intensity and
  use spatial clustering to identify hotspots where high outage exposure overlaps with
  vulnerable populations. The southeastern U.S., particularly Texas, Louisiana, and
  Florida, shows high-frequency outages alongside large concentrations of older adults
  and minority populations.
