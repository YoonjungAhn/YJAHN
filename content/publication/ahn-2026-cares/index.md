abstract: We develop the Care Resource Equity Score (CaRES), which integrates the Gini
  coefficient and the Location Quotient to measure both how evenly formal care jobs
  are distributed within a county and how concentrated they are. Using U.S. census
  tract data aggregated to all counties from 2009–2021, we classify counties into four
  typologies (Equitable Coverage, Concentrated Access, Even Desert, and Unequal Scarcity)
  across health care, education, and daily living sectors. Most counties fall into
  either Unequal Scarcity or Concentrated Access, and nonmetropolitan counties,
  especially small-town and rural ones, are disproportionately Even Deserts.
summary: CaRES combines the Gini coefficient and Location Quotient to map formal care
  employment equity across all U.S. counties, 2009–2021.
