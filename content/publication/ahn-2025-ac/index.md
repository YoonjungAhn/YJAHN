abstract: Air conditioning (AC) is a key protective factor against extreme heat, yet
  national AC information has only been available at broad geographic levels such as
  counties or metropolitan areas. We built a property-level dataset covering more than
  103 million housing units (73.6% of housing units in the contiguous U.S.) and used
  XGBoost models informed by housing characteristics, socioeconomic indicators, and
  environmental conditions (e.g., cooling degree days and elevation) to impute four
  AC types. The final product provides national coverage at census tract and ZIP code
  levels, with county and metropolitan summaries.
summary: A national, property-based dataset of residential air conditioning prevalence
  by AC type at census tract and ZIP code levels.
