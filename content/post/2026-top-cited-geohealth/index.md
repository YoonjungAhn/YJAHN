Excited to share that our paper, [**Comparing Approximated Heat Stress Measures Across the United States**](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023GH000923), has been recognized as a **Top Cited Article in *GeoHealth*** by Wiley (among work published between January 1 and December 31, 2024). 🏆

In this study, we compared three heat measures (HImax, WBGT<sub>Bernard</sub>, and WBGT<sub>Liljegren</sub>) derived from gridded weather datasets (ERA5-Land, PRISM, and Daymet) with ground-based data.

I’m especially grateful to my wonderful coauthors, Robbie M. Parks and Cascade Tuholske, for their collaboration and support throughout this project.
